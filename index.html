ABOUT CAFE WORLD PUBLICATION

Connecting Good Books With Good Readers

Cafe World Publication is a Bathinda-based publication working to bring meaningful literature, Punjabi books and literary works to readers across India and abroad.

For more than 10 years, we have been working with books, publishing and printing, with a focus on literature that carries ideas, stories and values worth sharing.

Our collection includes classical books, Punjabi literature, translated works and Lehndi Punjab poetry, along with other meaningful literary works.

Our Vision We believe that books can shape thoughts, preserve culture and connect people with ideas. Our aim is simple — to help good literature reach more readers.

More Than Just Books

At Cafe World Publication, our work goes beyond simply bringing books to readers. We are committed to promoting meaningful literature, supporting writers, and making Punjabi and literary works accessible to readers across India and abroad.
